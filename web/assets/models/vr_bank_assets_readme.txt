VR Bank Reference Assets (.glb)

Assets included:
1. vr_bank_teller_desk_counter.glb
   - Modular teller desk/counter with front LED strip, glass guard, monitor, signature pad, brochure holder, and generic bank-signage placeholders.
   - Approx. dimensions: 4.45m wide x 0.82m deep x 1.91m high.
   - Suggested position in room: x=0, y=0, z=0.

2. vr_bank_atm.glb
   - Wall-recessed ATM with screen, keypad, card reader, cash slot, side buttons, header, and blue wall frame.
   - Approx. dimensions: 1.22m wide x 0.48m deep x 2.28m high.
   - Suggested position: right wall alcove; orient front toward user/player.

3. vr_bank_teller_npc.glb
   - Simple reference teller NPC, standing, with suit, shirt, head/hair, arms/hands, badge, and floor interaction marker.
   - Approx. height: 1.85m.
   - Suggested position: behind teller desk, facing the user.

Notes:
- These are lightweight reference/blockout assets intended for VR coding and interaction prototyping, not final production art.
- Materials use simple color-coded PBR materials for quick readability in engines.
- For interaction systems, attach collider/trigger volumes to the ATM screen/buttons and the teller NPC/floor marker inside your game engine.
